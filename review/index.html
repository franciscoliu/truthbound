Product · policy-owner review

Review the rules before anyone enforces them.

Truthbound compiles a written policy into rules. Before those rules grade an AI agent, train a new hire or block an action, the person who owns the policy reads each rule in plain language, sees whether every condition rests on the customer's word or on the records, checks the cases it generates, and approves or corrects it.
