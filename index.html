AUTONOMY NEEDS PROOF

Prove your agent
follows the policy.

Truthbound compiles a written policy into an executable check, tests the agent on every branch of it, and enforces it while the agent works.

In our demo, a low-cost support agent approved a $4,986 refund the policy forbids. The same check stopped it, and let that cheap model match frontier models on every case.

WHY IT MATTERS

More autonomy.
More at stake.

An agent's decision can trigger a refund, a release or a promise to a customer. A written policy only protects you if the agent follows it on every branch, every time, and you can show that it did.

What Truthbound does, before
and after an agent release.

  1. 01Compile the policyPolicy text becomes an executable oracle, checked for completeness and conflicts.
  2. 02Test every branchEnumerate the outcomes, generate realistic cases, grade the agent against the oracle.
  3. 03Enforce at runtimeBlock forbidden actions and reject answers that contradict the policy.

SEE IT ON A REAL POLICY

A $4,986 mistake,
caught twice.

We compiled an airline cancellation policy, generated a case for each of its seven outcomes, and ran a low-cost support agent with real tools. It approved a refund the policy forbids. Testing every branch found it; the runtime check stopped it. With the check on, the same cheap model matched GPT-5.5 and Claude Opus 5.5 on every case, at about 2% of their cost.
